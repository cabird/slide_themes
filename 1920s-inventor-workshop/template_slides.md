## Template Types Found

### Template: Title (Full-Bleed Photo)
**Slides using this template:** 1, 17
**Background:** Full-bleed black-and-white photograph of a 1920s workshop/industrial scene. No visible color overlay -- the photograph itself is monochromatic. Slight natural vignetting from the photograph. Subtle dark gradient from bottom-left to ensure text legibility over busy image areas.
**Layout:** Full-width, full-height background image. Text anchored to the left side, vertically centered or slightly above center. Generous left margin (~8-9% from left edge). Text occupies roughly the left 55-60% of the slide width.
**Elements:**
- Speaker attribution line: small caps, light weight, positioned top-left above the headline (~7% from top, ~8% from left). Format: "[Speaker Name] / [Organization]". Letter-spacing is wide (tracked out).
- Headline: 2-3 lines of large bold text, left-aligned, positioned in the upper-left quadrant.
- On slide 17 (closing variant): headline is slightly lower and vertically centered. Below the headline, a subtitle line in regular weight, followed by a bold call-to-action or summary line. No speaker attribution line at top.
- Slide number: small, bottom-right corner, light gray on the photo.
**Typography:**
- Attribution: IBM Plex Sans, regular or light weight, ~14px equivalent, white, all-caps with wide letter-spacing (~0.15em)
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold/extra-bold, ~54-60px equivalent, white, normal case
- Subtitle (closing variant): IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~20px, white with slight reduced opacity (~80%)
- Bold summary line (closing variant): IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~20px, white
**Color tokens used:** White text only. Photo is grayscale.
**Decorative elements:** None -- the photograph provides all visual texture. Clean, unadorned typography over image.
**Notes:** This template REQUIRES a full-bleed black-and-white photograph. The image should have enough dark area or natural falloff on the left side to ensure white text remains legible. Slide 1 is the opening title; slide 17 is a closing variant with more body text and no top attribution. The closing variant feels more like a "statement over image" than a formal title card.

### Template: Dark Statement
**Slides using this template:** 2, 14
**Background:** Solid near-black (#1a1a1a to #111111). Completely flat, no image, no gradient.
**Layout:** Text block positioned in the left half of the slide, vertically centered (or slightly above center on slide 2). Generous left margin (~8%). Text block occupies roughly the left 55-60% of slide width. Large amount of negative space on the right side.
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps text with a vertical accent bar to its left, positioned above the headline. The accent bar is approximately 3-4px wide, ~18px tall, in a muted blue/steel color. Label is tracked-out uppercase.
- Headline: 2-3 lines of very large bold white text. This is a punchy declarative statement, not a descriptive title.
- Horizontal rule: a short horizontal line (~60px wide, ~2px thick) in a muted tone (steel gray or muted blue), positioned below the headline with moderate spacing, acting as a section divider.
- Supporting text (optional): below the horizontal rule, a single sentence in regular weight, smaller size. On slide 2 this is present; on slide 14 the space below the rule is left empty.
- Slide number: small, bottom-right, in a muted gray.
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular/medium, ~12-13px, white at reduced opacity (~60%), all-caps, letter-spacing ~0.15em
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, extra-bold, ~48-54px equivalent, pure white, normal case
- Supporting text: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~24-26px, white at ~85% opacity
**Color tokens used:** Near-black background, white text, steel/muted blue accent bar beside section label, muted gray horizontal rule
**Decorative elements:**
- Vertical accent bar: ~3-4px wide, ~18px tall, steel-blue color, positioned immediately to the left of the section label. This is a signature element of the theme used on both dark and light backgrounds.
- Horizontal rule: short (~60px), thin (~2px), muted gray/blue, used as a thought separator between headline and supporting text.
**Notes:** This is a high-impact statement slide. It works entirely without images. The extreme contrast (bright white bold text on near-black) creates dramatic emphasis. Best used for provocative claims, thesis statements, or section openers that need to land hard. The large negative space on the right is intentional -- it gives the statement room to breathe.

### Template: Light Statement
**Slides using this template:** 4
**Background:** Solid warm light gray (#f0edea or similar -- slightly warm, not pure gray). No image.
**Layout:** Text positioned in the left half, vertically starting in the upper third. Generous left margin (~8%). Massive negative space in the right half and bottom of the slide.
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps with vertical accent bar to its left, positioned at top-left (~5% from top, ~8% from left). Accent bar is dark gray or black.
- Horizontal rule: short thick dash (~50px wide, ~3px), dark/black, positioned above the headline with moderate spacing. This is different from the dark statement template where the rule appears below the headline.
- Headline: 2-3 lines of very large bold text in near-black. Punchy, declarative statement.
- Supporting text: 1-2 lines of regular weight text in medium gray, positioned below the headline with generous spacing (~40px gap).
- Slide number: bottom-right, muted gray.
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~12-13px, dark gray (#333), all-caps, wide letter-spacing
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, extra-bold, ~48-54px, near-black (#1a1a1a), normal case
- Supporting text: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~20-22px, medium gray (#555)
**Color tokens used:** Warm light gray background, near-black text, dark gray secondary text, dark accent bar
**Decorative elements:**
- Vertical accent bar beside section label (dark gray/black variant)
- Horizontal dash/rule above headline (decorative separator)
**Notes:** This is the light-background counterpart to the Dark Statement template. Same structural purpose (bold claim with supporting context) but inverted tonality. The warm gray background avoids the harshness of pure white while maintaining the industrial, paper-like feel. The horizontal rule above the headline (rather than below) subtly distinguishes it from the dark variant.

### Template: Transition (Full-Bleed Photo)
**Slides using this template:** 5, 7, 10
**Background:** Full-bleed black-and-white photograph. The images show workshop tools, instruments, and materials in the 1920s industrial style. No explicit overlay, but photographs are chosen/processed to have sufficient dark areas for text placement.
**Layout:** Text anchored to the left side, vertically centered. Text occupies roughly the left 40-50% of the slide. The right portion of the image is allowed to show through without text.
**Elements:**
- Section label: very small caps text above the headline, white, reduced opacity. On some slides preceded by a subtle vertical accent bar. The label names the section being introduced (e.g., "PREPARE", "VERIFY", "ENABLE").
- Headline: 1-2 lines of large bold white text. Short, aphoristic statement (e.g., "[Concept] is [quality]."). This is NOT a section title but rather a thematic statement for the section.
- Subtitle (optional): on slide 10, a single smaller line appears below the headline providing additional context. Light weight, slightly reduced opacity.
- Slide number: bottom-right, small, white, sometimes hard to see over the photo.
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~11-12px, white at ~50-60% opacity, all-caps, wide letter-spacing
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~40-46px, pure white
- Subtitle: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~18px, white at ~80% opacity
**Color tokens used:** White text only. Grayscale photograph.
**Decorative elements:** None -- the photograph IS the decoration. Clean type over image.
**Notes:** These are section transition slides. They mark the boundary between major sections of the talk. Each uses a different B&W workshop photograph but maintains the same text positioning pattern. The key distinction from the Title template: transition slides have a section label (not speaker attribution), the headline is shorter and more aphoristic (a principle, not a topic), and there is no subtitle block. The photograph should have a clear zone of relatively even tone on the left side for text legibility. Slides 5 and 7 have a very similar structure (label + short headline). Slide 10 adds a subtitle line beneath, making it a slight variation.

### Template: Figure (Light Background with Diagram)
**Slides using this template:** 3, 8, 13
**Background:** Solid warm light gray (#f0edea). No background image.
**Layout:** Title block at top-left, followed by a full-width or near-full-width diagram/figure area in the middle, with optional supporting text below the diagram. Generous margins on all sides (~8% left/right, ~5% top).
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps with vertical accent bar, top-left, dark text
- Headline: large bold text below the section label, left-aligned
- Horizontal rule: thin line (~1px, gray) spanning roughly 35-40% of the slide width, positioned below the headline as an underline/separator
- Figure caption: monospaced label above the diagram (e.g., "FIG. 03 -- [DESCRIPTION]"), in small caps monospace font, gray. This is enclosed in a subtle dashed-border rectangle on slide 3, or positioned as a label.
- Diagram area: the central focus of the slide. Contains boxes, arrows, flow lines, and labels. The diagrams use:
  - Thin-bordered rectangles with left-edge accent bars (colored: navy, teal, indigo)
  - Arrows: solid dark arrows (triangular heads) for primary flow; dashed gray arrows for feedback loops
  - Step numbers in circles (on slide 8)
  - Monospaced text for technical labels and artifact names
  - Bold colored labels for phase/step names (PREPARE, EXECUTE, VERIFY, etc.)
  - Regular weight for descriptive text within boxes
  - Small pill-shaped badges for metadata (e.g., "COST: LOW")
  - Horizontal colored bars (top of boxes) indicating step identity
- Supporting statement: bold text below the diagram, preceded by a short horizontal accent bar (colored), serving as a takeaway or summary
- Italic quote/annotation: smaller italic text, either below the figure or below the summary statement
- Blueprint cartouche (slide 8): bottom-right corner, a small bordered box with monospaced text reading "SHEET: V-03 / SCALE: 1:1 / DRAWN BY: [TOOL]" -- a playful industrial/engineering reference
- Slide number: bottom-right, small gray
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~12-13px, dark gray, all-caps, wide letter-spacing
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~40-44px, near-black
- Figure caption: IBM Plex Mono, regular, ~11px, medium gray, all-caps, letter-spacing
- Diagram phase labels: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~14-16px, colored (navy/teal/indigo)
- Diagram body text: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~14-15px, dark gray
- Diagram monospaced labels: IBM Plex Mono, regular, ~11-12px, gray
- Summary statement: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~22-26px, near-black
- Italic annotation: IBM Plex Serif (italic), ~16-18px, medium gray
**Color tokens used:** Warm light gray background, near-black text, navy (#1e3a5f) for accent bars and labels, teal (#0d7377) for accent bars and labels, indigo (#3d3b8e) for accent bars, steel gray for borders and secondary elements
**Decorative elements:**
- Dashed-line borders around the overall figure area (engineering drawing style)
- Left-edge colored accent bars on content boxes (3-4px wide vertical bars)
- Circled step numbers
- Arrow connectors (solid and dashed)
- Blueprint cartouche (bottom-right engineering reference block)
- Short colored horizontal accent bar before summary text
**Notes:** This is the most complex template in the system. It is designed for process flows, system diagrams, and architectural overviews. The engineering-drawing aesthetic (dashed borders, figure captions, cartouche) reinforces the 1920s workshop/blueprint metaphor. The diagram content is highly variable -- slide 3 shows a 4-box flow with a feedback element, slide 8 shows a linear 4-step pipeline with a feedback loop, and slide 13 shows a 4-column progression. The template accommodates these variations through flexible diagram placement. On slide 6, a related variant appears under the "Comparison" template below.

### Template: Comparison (Two-Column Light)
**Slides using this template:** 6
**Background:** Solid warm light gray (#f0edea). No image.
**Layout:** Title block at top-left. Below the title, two side-by-side columns, each containing a vertical flow diagram. A summary statement and italic annotation below the columns. Roughly 50/50 column split with a visible gutter between them.
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps with vertical accent bar, top-left
- Headline: large bold text, left-aligned
- Horizontal rule: thin gray line below headline (~35% width)
- Two named columns, each with:
  - Bold all-caps column heading (e.g., "[SYSTEM A]", "[SYSTEM B]")
  - Subtitle description in regular gray text
  - A vertical flow of 4-5 labeled rows, each row being a box with a bold monospaced label on the left and regular description text on the right. Rows are separated by small downward arrows.
  - Left-edge accent bars on each row (dark gray)
- "MANIFEST" callout box: positioned to the right of the second column, connected by dashed lines. Contains bold heading and regular description text. Dashed border.
- Summary statement: bold text, preceded by short dark horizontal rule, positioned below both columns
- Italic annotation: IBM Plex Serif italic, below the summary
- Slide number: bottom-right
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~12px, dark gray, all-caps
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~40-44px, near-black
- Column headings: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~14-15px, near-black, all-caps, letter-spacing
- Column subtitles: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~14px, medium gray
- Row labels: IBM Plex Mono or IBM Plex Sans bold, ~12-13px, dark gray, all-caps
- Row descriptions: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~14-15px, dark gray
- Summary: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~22-24px, near-black
- Italic annotation: IBM Plex Serif italic, ~16px, medium gray
**Color tokens used:** Warm light gray bg, near-black text, medium gray secondary, dark gray accent bars
**Decorative elements:**
- Vertical accent bars on each row's left edge
- Small downward-pointing arrows between rows
- Dashed-border callout box with connecting dashed lines
- Short horizontal rule as thought-separator before summary
**Notes:** This is a specialized variant of the Figure template, optimized for side-by-side comparison of two systems or processes. The parallel vertical flows make structural similarities and differences immediately visible. The "MANIFEST" callout box shows how an annotation can break out of the column grid. Could be generalized as a "Two-Column Flow Comparison" template.

### Template: 50/50 Split (Text Left, Photo Right)
**Slides using this template:** 9, 11, 15, 16
**Background:** Left half is solid warm light gray (#f0edea); right half is a full-bleed black-and-white photograph. The split is approximately 50/50, though some slides vary slightly (45/55 or 40/60).
**Layout:** Clean vertical division at the horizontal midpoint. Text content is entirely within the left panel. The photograph fills the right panel edge-to-edge, top-to-bottom. No overlap between text and image zones.
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps with vertical accent bar, positioned top-left of the left panel (~5-6% from top, ~4-5% from left edge of slide or ~8% of the left panel width)
- Headline: bold text, 1-3 lines, left-aligned within the left panel
- Body text: regular weight paragraphs below the headline. Can be a single paragraph or multiple separated paragraphs/items.
  - On slide 9: short introductory paragraph, then a set of individual statements as separate lines/paragraphs
  - On slide 11: two paragraphs, with the second one being a bold pull-quote/summary separated by a short horizontal rule
  - On slide 15: many short single-line statements forming a list without bullets (each on its own line, regular weight), creating a principles/list layout
  - On slide 16: section label, headline, then body paragraphs with an italic closing statement
- Horizontal rule (optional): short line separating body text from a concluding bold statement
- Bold summary line (optional): heavier-weight closing thought at the bottom of the text block
- Italic annotation (optional): italic text as a closing remark
- Thin vertical separator line: a subtle 1px gray line at the boundary between the text panel and the photo panel, visible on some slides
- Slide number: bottom-right, over the photograph, small white or gray
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~12px, dark gray, all-caps, wide letter-spacing
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~30-36px, near-black (smaller than the statement/figure templates because it shares space with body text)
- Body text: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~15-17px, dark gray (#333), line-height ~1.5
- Bold summary: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~16-18px, near-black
- Italic annotation: IBM Plex Serif italic, ~15-16px, medium gray
**Color tokens used:** Warm light gray (left panel), grayscale photo (right panel), near-black text, medium gray secondary text, steel/indigo accent bar on section label
**Decorative elements:**
- Vertical accent bar beside section label
- Optional short horizontal rule as thought-separator within body text
- Thin vertical line at the panel boundary (subtle structural element)
**Notes:** This is the workhorse content template for the presentation. It pairs explanation text with atmospheric photography. The photographs do not illustrate the content literally -- they maintain the 1920s workshop mood. Variations within this template:
- **Narrative variant** (slides 9, 11, 16): headline + 1-2 paragraphs + optional bold summary. Standard expository use.
- **List variant** (slide 15): headline + many short single-line items forming an enumerated-by-line-break list. Used for principles, guidelines, or takeaways. Text is denser and smaller.
- **Reversed variant**: see slide 12 below (photo on LEFT, text on RIGHT).

### Template: 50/50 Split (Photo Left, Text Right)
**Slides using this template:** 12
**Background:** Left half is a full-bleed black-and-white photograph; right half is solid warm light gray (#f0edea). This is the mirror of the standard 50/50 split.
**Layout:** The photograph fills the left panel edge-to-edge. All text content is in the right panel. A thin vertical accent line (colored, not gray) appears at the left edge of the text panel, running nearly the full height.
**Elements:**
- Section label: small caps with vertical accent bar, positioned at top of the right panel
- Headline: large bold text, 1-2 lines
- Body text: regular weight paragraph(s) below headline
- Structured data block: a vertical stack of labeled rows, each with a monospaced all-caps label on the left and regular description on the right. Thin horizontal rules separate each row. Connected by small downward arrows.
- Monospaced detail section: below the structured block, a bold all-caps label followed by monospaced descriptions of tools/commands. This creates a "technical reference" feel.
- Thin vertical accent line: a colored line (~2px, teal or indigo) running along the left edge of the text panel, from top to near-bottom. This visually anchors the text panel and references the engineering-drawing border motif.
- Slide number: bottom-right, gray
**Typography:**
- Section label: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~12px, gray, all-caps, wide letter-spacing
- Headline: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~36-40px, near-black
- Body text: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~16-17px, dark gray
- Structured row labels: IBM Plex Mono, regular, ~11-12px, medium gray, all-caps
- Structured row descriptions: IBM Plex Sans, regular, ~15-16px, dark gray
- Tool labels: IBM Plex Sans, bold, ~13px, dark gray/near-black, all-caps
- Tool descriptions: IBM Plex Mono, regular, ~13px, gray
**Color tokens used:** Warm light gray (right panel), grayscale photo (left panel), near-black text, medium gray, teal or indigo for the vertical accent line
**Decorative elements:**
- Full-height thin colored vertical line at the text panel's left edge
- Vertical accent bar beside section label
- Small downward arrows between structured rows
- Thin horizontal rules separating structured data rows
**Notes:** This reversed-split layout creates visual variety when used alongside the standard (text-left, photo-right) 50/50 splits. The presence of structured data (label-value rows, monospaced tool references) makes this a good fit for technical reference content. The colored vertical line running the full height of the text panel is a distinctive element not seen in the standard 50/50 split.

## Common Design System Elements

These elements appear consistently across multiple templates:

### Vertical Accent Bar (Section Label Marker)
- A small vertical bar (~3-4px wide, ~16-20px tall) positioned immediately to the left of the section label text
- Color varies by context: steel-blue on dark backgrounds, dark gray or colored (navy/teal/indigo) on light backgrounds
- Always accompanies the all-caps section label

### Section Label
- All-caps text with wide letter-spacing (~0.12-0.15em)
- IBM Plex Sans, regular weight, ~11-13px
- Always preceded by the vertical accent bar
- Positioned at the top-left of the content area
- Names the current section or subsection (e.g., "THE PROBLEM", "PREPARE", "VERIFY / THE FRONTIER")
- Hierarchical labels use a centered dot or middle dot separator (e.g., "ENABLE / SESSION MEMORY EXPLORER")

### Horizontal Rule (Thought Separator)
- A short horizontal line, typically 50-70px wide, ~2px thick
- Positioned between the headline/main statement and supporting text
- Dark gray or muted colored
- Acts as a visual breath between the primary claim and its explanation

### Slide Number
- Small text (~10-11px), positioned in the bottom-right corner
- On light backgrounds: medium gray
- On dark backgrounds or photos: light gray or white at reduced opacity
- Consistent position across all templates: approximately 93% from left, 95% from top

### Monospaced Technical Labels
- IBM Plex Mono for all technical identifiers, code references, artifact names, and figure captions
- Slightly smaller than body text
- Often in medium gray rather than near-black
- All-caps with letter-spacing for figure captions and category labels

### Italic Annotations
- IBM Plex Serif (italic) used for editorial asides, quotes, and reflective commentary
- Medium gray color, slightly smaller than body text
- Positioned as the final element in a text block, often after a summary statement
- Creates a human, editorial voice distinct from the factual body text

### Color Accent Usage
Colors are used sparingly and with clear semantic meaning:
- **Navy (#1e3a5f)**: Primary accent for diagram elements, box accent bars
- **Teal (#0d7377)**: Secondary accent for diagram labels, vertical panel borders
- **Indigo (#3d3b8e)**: Tertiary accent for section labels, step markers
- **Steel gray (#6b7b8d)**: Borders, secondary structural elements, muted labels
- Colors appear ONLY in accent bars, diagram labels, and small structural elements -- never as backgrounds or large fills
